About FoodBridge AI

Bridging Surplus Food with Those Who Need It Most

FoodBridge AI is a smart, community-driven platform that connects surplus food from donors — restaurants, households, events, and businesses — with NGOs, volunteers, and communities facing food insecurity. By using intelligent matching and real-time logistics, we ensure good food reaches people instead of landfills.

Our ecosystem brings together donors, NGOs, and volunteer pickup partners to coordinate safe, timely food rescue operations. Every donation is tracked, matched, and delivered with transparency.

Our mission: Reduce food waste and hunger by building the fastest, most reliable food rescue network — powered by AI, driven by compassion.
